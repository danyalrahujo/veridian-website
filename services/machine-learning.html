Service — Machine Learning

Predictive models that plug into how decisions actually get made

We build classification, regression, and ranking models designed around the operational workflow they need to influence — not just the offline accuracy metric.

Schedule a ConsultationView FAQs
The Problem

Models that score well offline often change nothing operationally

A model with strong offline accuracy is only useful if it reaches the person or system making the decision, at the moment the decision is made, in a format they can act on.

  • ✕Models built without a clear downstream consumer
  • ✕No plan for retraining as data distributions shift
  • ✕Predictions that arrive too late to influence the decision
Our Approach

We design backward from the decision, forward to the data

Every model engagement starts by mapping the exact decision it needs to influence, then works backward to the features, latency requirements, and retraining cadence that decision demands.

  • ✓Feature pipelines built for the latency the decision requires
  • ✓Automated retraining triggered by monitored data drift
  • ✓Model outputs delivered inside the tool the decision-maker already uses

Delivery Process
