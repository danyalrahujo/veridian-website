Service — Data Analytics

Dashboards executives actually open on a Monday morning

We design governed semantic layers and executive dashboards that answer the specific questions your leadership team asks in the room — not a generic KPI wall.

Schedule a ConsultationView FAQs
The Problem

Most dashboards get built once and opened rarely

Dashboards built without a specific decision in mind tend to accumulate metrics no one owns, and leadership quietly falls back to ad hoc spreadsheet requests.

  • ✕Dozens of dashboards with no clear owner or decision attached
  • ✕Conflicting numbers across BI tools due to no shared semantic layer
  • ✕Reports that take days to refresh, arriving too late to act on
Our Approach

One governed semantic layer, purpose-built dashboards on top

We build a single source-of-truth semantic model, then design dashboards around the specific decisions each audience needs to make — refreshed on a cadence that matches how fast the business moves.

  • ✓A governed semantic layer feeding every downstream dashboard
  • ✓Dashboards scoped to a named decision and named owner
  • ✓Refresh cadences matched to decision velocity, not default schedules
