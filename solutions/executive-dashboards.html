Solution — Executive Dashboards

A live decision cockpit for your leadership team

A governed, single-pane view of the metrics your executive team actually reviews — connected to live data, not a monthly export.

Overview

What this solution does

Executive Dashboards sit on top of your governed semantic layer, surfacing the handful of metrics each leadership function actually reviews on a recurring cadence, with drill-down available for anyone who needs the detail behind a number.

Business Value

Why it's worth building

  • ✓Replace manual monthly deck assembly with a live view
  • ✓Give every executive function one trusted number, not five conflicting ones
  • ✓Surface exceptions automatically instead of waiting for a review meeting

Workflow

How it operates end to end

Step 01

Metric definition

Agree the specific metrics each executive function needs, and who owns each definition.

Step 02

Semantic layer connection

Connect the dashboard to a governed, single source-of-truth data model.

Step 03

Dashboard design

Design views for a five-minute leadership scan, with drill-down beneath.

Step 04

Rollout & alerting

Launch with threshold-based alerts for the metrics that need proactive attention.
